This preview fixes missing listening controls and battery readings, and adds automatic AirPods detection.

- The packet decoder handles repeated AAP headers, including fragmented notifications, instead of discarding their contents.
- Valid battery percentages survive an unknown charging status. A previously received percentage is kept with a Last reading label when the device stops reporting it, rather than being erased.
- Notification setup follows the initial AirPods response and retries after connection. Battery readings can also be refreshed from the battery card.
- Noise-control commands remain available for Pro, Max, and ANC models while the current mode is being read. Commands still wait for a device report before selecting the new mode.
- Android’s connected A2DP devices are checked automatically when Pods opens and while its process is running. Previously chosen AirPods are remembered by address, including renamed devices. Ambiguous pairs require selection, and unrelated headphones are not selected.

This is a standalone preview APK and does not require Expo Go or a Metro server. Pair your AirPods in Android Bluetooth settings and allow Pods Nearby devices access. Already connected AirPods will be detected after permission is granted.

Auto-detection operates while the app process is running and on app launch. This preview does not run a persistent service that wakes an app stopped by Android. Hardware confirmation of the corrected readings and commands on Pixel 9 is still required; parser and selection tests do not substitute for physical-device validation.

Validation before submission: native Kotlin compilation, 13 protocol and device-selection tests, two model/control-visibility tests, lint, and TypeScript checks passed. Physical-device confirmation is pending.
